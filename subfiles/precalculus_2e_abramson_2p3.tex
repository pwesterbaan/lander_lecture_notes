  \section{2.3: Modeling with Linear Functions}
  \begin{ex*}
    Elan is a college student who plans to spend a summer in Seattle. Elan has saved \$$3,500$ for the trip and anticipates spending \$$400$ each week on rent, food, and activities. Write a linear model to represent this scenario. Identify and interpret:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item the rate of change
      \item the initial amount
      \item the $x$-intercept
    \end{itemize}
    \vspace*{\stretch{1}}
  \end{ex*}
  \pagebreak
